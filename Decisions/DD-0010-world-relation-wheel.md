# DD-0010 — World Relation Wheel

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Affected documents:** [World Relation Wheel and Flavor](../Procedural/World_Relation_Wheel_and_Flavor.md), [Lore Map and Affinity Wheels](../Procedural/Lore_Map_and_Affinity_Wheels.md), [PlaneGuardian GDD](../GDD/PlaneGuardian_GDD.md)

## Context

Ethos describes what state a card values but not how it acts upon reality. Procedural abilities and names need a separate semantic model for maintaining identity, changing form, bringing things into existence, and removing them from existence.

## Decision

World Relation uses the axes Preservation–Transformation and Creation–Annihilation. Its primary regions are Restoration, Genesis, Excision, and Dissolution.

Preservation maintains identity or continuity. Transformation changes identity or state. Creation brings something new into existence. Annihilation removes something from existence and is distinct from ordinary damage, destruction, decay, and Death.

World Relation supplies process and consequence vocabulary. Ethos supplies values, while Elemental Affinity and other wheels supply substance, imagery, and expression.

## Rationale

- Give procedural abilities and names a coherent vocabulary of action.
- Permit combinations such as Life-aligned Annihilation or Death-aligned Restoration without contradiction.
- Distinguish true erasure from damage, decomposition, and mortality.
- Support card-type-specific language for places, entities, facilities, Actions, and Battle Events.

## Consequences

- Summoning must distinguish creation from transportation.
- Restoration cannot bypass rarity, global supply, or ownership rules.
- Annihilation terminology must be reserved for genuine ontological removal.
- Exact vocabulary metadata and mechanical thresholds remain future work.
