# DD-0012 — Magical Tradition Wheel

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Affected documents:** [PlaneGuardian GDD](../GDD/PlaneGuardian_GDD.md), [Lore Map and Affinity Wheels](../Procedural/Lore_Map_and_Affinity_Wheels.md), [Magical Tradition Wheel and Flavor](../Procedural/Magical_Tradition_Wheel_and_Flavor.md)

## Context

Cards that produce similar results need coherent differences in how those
results are achieved. Elemental Affinity describes what resonates within a
card, while the other established wheels describe values, changes to reality,
and mode of presence. None describes practice, discipline, or method.

The wheel's center also needs positive meaning. A centered coordinate cannot
merely signify generic magic, because mundane labor, craft, training, and
logistics are important identities and useful balancing tools.

## Decision

Magical Tradition uses two axes:

- **Arcane--Primal:** abstract and constructed knowledge versus inherited,
  living, ancestral, or world-bound knowledge;
- **Controlled--Instinctive:** prepared, repeatable discipline versus
  immediate, intuitive, adaptive expression.

Its primary regions are:

- **Artifice:** Arcane and Controlled;
- **Sorcery:** Arcane and Instinctive;
- **Ritual:** Primal and Controlled;
- **Wilding:** Primal and Instinctive.

The exact center is **Mundane**: the result is achieved without supernatural
technique through labor, tools, training, logistics, ingenuity, patience, and
time. A high-Salience center makes this method central to the card's identity;
low Salience means the method is irrelevant.

Mundane effects tend to cost fewer magical or exotic resources but require
longer completion times or cooldowns. This is a balancing tendency rather
than a universal formula. Mundane does not imply weak, common, ordinary in
quality, or aesthetically non-fantastical.

## Rationale

The wheel can distinguish five coherent ways to reach the same result without
duplicating Elemental Affinity or card type. It gives nonmagical cards an
affirmative identity and creates a useful economic trade: magic compresses
effort into resources, while mundane work pays more of its cost in time.

## Consequences

- Tradition vocabulary should contribute methods, verbs, instruments, and
  failure modes more often than subjects.
- Mundane cards may represent exceptional craft, armies, medicine,
  engineering, agriculture, or industry without being treated as lesser cards.
- Controlled methods tend toward predictable execution; Instinctive methods
  may be more adaptive, conditional, or variable, subject to later balancing.
- Exact cost curves, cooldown modifiers, and whether labor capacity becomes a
  resource remain open design work.
