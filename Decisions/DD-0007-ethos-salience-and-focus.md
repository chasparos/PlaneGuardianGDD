# DD-0007 — Ethos Salience and Focus

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Affected documents:** [Ethos Wheel and Naming Vocabulary](../Procedural/Ethos_Wheel_and_Naming_Vocabulary.md), [Lore Map and Affinity Wheels](../Procedural/Lore_Map_and_Affinity_Wheels.md)

## Context

Per-axis Salience could distinguish which Ethos axis most motivates a card, including different Champions of Balance. Applying it to every two-axis wheel would multiply procedural and naming states while often producing differences that players cannot perceive.

## Decision

Each wheel retains one Salience value. Complex cards may receive an optional Focus that narrows interpretation without becoming another independent naming branch. Ethos Focus may be Order–Chaos, Life–Death, Holistic, Center, or Cycle.

High-Salience, low-Extremity cards may use relationships and Focus to represent active balance, synthesis, conflict, alternation, suppression, or paradox. Focus and exceptional relationships consume complexity budget.

## Rationale

- Keep the common procedural model compact and understandable.
- Preserve special axis-specific identities where players can perceive them.
- Support distinct Champions of Balance without generating per-axis Salience for every card.
- Bound naming, ability, and art branches through Complexity.

## Consequences

- Coordinate position carries ordinary relative axis influence.
- Focus is absent from most simple cards.
- Naming treats Ethos as one influence and uses Focus only to constrain eligible vocabulary.
- Mechanical rules for counterweight and balance effects remain to be specified.
