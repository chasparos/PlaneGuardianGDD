# DD-0005 — Creative Stewardship of Unique Cards

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Affected document:** [PlaneGuardian GDD](../GDD/PlaneGuardian_GDD.md)

## Context

Discovering a unique card should allow the community to contribute to the living mythology of a Universe without compromising deterministic mechanics, safety, or historical attribution.

## Decision

Every unique card has a deterministic fallback name and presentation. Its first discoverer receives nomination rights for a display name and may propose artwork. Player contributions affect presentation only and require provenance, automated checks, community review, reporting, moderation, and appeal. Discoverer, contributor, and current-owner identities remain separate in the card's history.

## Rationale

- Reward unique discovery with meaningful creative participation.
- Let the Universe accumulate community-authored history.
- Preserve a safe fallback if a contribution is rejected or removed.
- Prevent ownership transfers or moderation from erasing attribution.

## Consequences

- Player artwork requires explicit ownership, licensing, attribution, and content rules.
- Community voting informs review but does not automatically override moderation.
- Approved presentation and its version history may persist if the card becomes Eternal.
- Naming and art cannot change deterministic mechanics.
