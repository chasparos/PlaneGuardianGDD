# Attachment Slots Production Guide

- **Status:** Initial guide derived from approved concept
- **Reference:** [Main Stage Concept 01](../Concepts/Main_Stage/README.md)

## Visual role

An empty slot communicates available capacity. It must not imply that the
attached structure, enchantment, lair, or other feature already exists.

## Default presentation

- Integrate a small flat clearing into the generated heightmap.
- Mark it with a low foundation, pedestal, mounting plinth, or equivalent
  biome-appropriate treatment.
- Preserve a shared circular rim glyph or structural motif across biomes.
- Keep it lower and quieter than every occupied landmark.
- Avoid adding slot markers to Lands that do not mechanically possess slots.

Possible material variations include cut stone, packed earth, roots, ice,
carved wood, bone, or restrained magical geometry. These variations must not
obscure the common interaction language.

## Occupied state

An attached card places its primary landmark on the foundation and may add a
small supporting composition nearby. The host biome remains visible. Removal
or cooldown effects should not require regenerating the island heightmap.

## Validation questions

1. Is the empty slot recognizable without opening the Land panel?
2. Does it look unoccupied rather than unfinished or destroyed?
3. Does the occupied landmark remain attributable to its attached card?
4. Can the same slot transform across biome materials without losing meaning?
