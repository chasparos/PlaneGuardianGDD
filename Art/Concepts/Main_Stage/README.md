## What this establishes

- A three-quarter tabletop view framed by an ornate but subordinate interface.
- A central Seed surrounded by discrete floating hex Lands and simple bridges.
- Mostly wild Lands whose identities come from height, topography, and two or
  three large silhouette landmarks.
- Civilized development as an exception supplied by biome identity or attached
  cards rather than decoration intrinsic to every Land.
- Visible empty attachment slots represented by small flat foundations or
  pedestals integrated into the terrain.
- Crisp island silhouettes over a quiet, low-contrast astral Void.
- A painterly high-fantasy treatment based on broad forms rather than
  semi-realistic surface detail.

## Illustrative, not binding

- The precise UI arrangement, proportions, and ornament are not final.
- The shown biomes, cards, bridge count, and Seed design are examples.
- The image is more polished than the first playable implementation needs to
  be and is not a required asset-density target.
- Atmospheric mist or smoke may later soften selected boundaries, but it is a
  separate rendering layer and must not conceal the underlying topology.
- Repeated cliff undersides and circular slot foundations require modular
  variation in production.

## Production implications

The pre-alpha should prove the direction with a controlled modular kit rather
than bespoke islands. It needs several heightmap families, interchangeable
cliff and underside profiles, biome surfaces, a small silhouette-landmark
library, recognizable empty-slot treatments, and separate atmosphere and
Plane-state layers.

## Generation history

This concept was generated and refined through three passes:

1. establish the Main Stage composition, ornate UI, floating island ring, and
   painterly astral-tabletop mood;
2. remove excessive settlement detail, make most Lands wild, emphasize
   heightmap topography, expose attachment foundations, and quiet the Void;
3. sharpen island-to-Void edges and add varied rear-horizon natural landmarks
   to flatter Lands.

The final refinement explicitly preserved the interface, camera, Seed, six
surrounding Lands, simple bridges, cultivated exception, and wild character.
It prohibited additional towns, ruins, towers, characters, combat, readable
text, edge mist, and fine-detail clutter.
