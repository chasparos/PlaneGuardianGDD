# Ethos Wheel and Naming Vocabulary

- **Status:** Foundational design with starter vocabulary
- **Related decision:** [DD-0007](../Decisions/DD-0007-ethos-salience-and-focus.md)
- **Related documents:** [Lore Map and Affinity Wheels](Lore_Map_and_Affinity_Wheels.md), [PlaneGuardian GDD](../GDD/PlaneGuardian_GDD.md)

## Purpose

Elemental Affinity describes what kind of power a card embodies. Ethos describes the state of existence it values. It influences behavior, abilities, military doctrine, naming, art, and lore without becoming a simple good–evil alignment.

## Axes

```text
Order <-> Chaos
Life  <-> Death
```

- **Order:** structure, law, repetition, hierarchy, predictability, and restraint.
- **Chaos:** freedom, spontaneity, mutation, adaptation, possibility, and disruption.
- **Life:** growth, propagation, vitality, appetite, birth, and biological emergence.
- **Death:** endings, mortality, decomposition, stillness, ancestry, and return.

Order may become tyranny, Chaos may enable liberation, Life may become uncontrolled consumption, and Death may provide mercy or renewal. Constructive and destructive expression comes from context and other wheels, especially World Relation.

## Primary regions

| Region | Working name | Constructive expressions | Destructive expressions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order + Life | Cultivation | Healing, stewardship, sanctuary, civilization | Forced conformity, controlled breeding, perfection through removal |
| Chaos + Life | Wild Growth | Adaptation, fertility, wilderness, diversity | Infestation, ravenous proliferation, uncontrolled mutation |
| Order + Death | Rite | Ancestry, funerary law, peaceful endings, remembrance | Eternal servitude, sterile stagnation, refusal to release the dead |
| Chaos + Death | Dissolution | Decomposition, scavenging, clearing space for renewal | Plague, entropy, indiscriminate ruin, collapse |

Axis-centered regions describe Structure, Flux, Vitality, and Mortality without a strong commitment on the other axis. A card can therefore express pure law, change, life, or ending without being forced into a quadrant.

## Salience, relationships, and Focus

Ethos retains one Salience value for the entire wheel. The coordinate already expresses the relative contribution of both axes for ordinary cards. Generating Salience per axis would add states that are difficult for players to perceive and would multiply naming and ability branches.

Complex cards may receive an optional **Focus**:

- **Order–Chaos:** emphasizes structure, freedom, and their balance;
- **Life–Death:** emphasizes vitality, mortality, and their cycle;
- **Holistic:** treats the complete wheel as one doctrine;
- **Center:** emphasizes neutral ground or the stillpoint;
- **Cycle:** emphasizes movement and succession among states.

Focus consumes complexity budget and should affect a name only when it creates a perceptible identity. Relationship modes such as Equilibrium, Synthesis, Conflict, Alternation, Suppression, and Paradox remain separate from Focus.

## Champions of Balance

A Champion of Balance has low Ethos Extremity, high Ethos Salience, an appropriate relationship such as Equilibrium or Correction, and optionally a Focus. It is not indecisive or automatically benevolent. It actively counteracts dominance:

- introduces Chaos into tyranny;
- imposes Order upon collapse;
- restores Life to sterile stillness;
- brings Death to endless, consuming growth.

Order–Chaos, Life–Death, and Holistic Focus produce different Champions. Their effects may scale with a target's distance from equilibrium or express the semantic counterweight to the target's dominant Ethos.

## Starter vocabulary

| Region | Concepts | Titles and nouns | Descriptors and verbs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order + Life | cultivation, stewardship, sanctuary, lineage | Cultivator, Steward, Gardener, Shepherd, Conservator | cultivated, ordered, fruitful; heal, govern, prune, refine |
| Chaos + Life | wilderness, adaptation, mutation, hunger | Wildling, Brood, Swarm, Shifter, Seedbearer | wild, fecund, rampant; bloom, spawn, mutate, overgrow |
| Order + Death | mortality, ancestry, judgment, rest | Ancestor, Judge, Gravekeeper, Ferryman, Last Witness | solemn, final, entombed; inter, conclude, remember, release |
| Chaos + Death | decay, entropy, ruin, carrion | Scavenger, Ravager, Rotbringer, Devourer, Ashwalker | festering, broken, blighted; rot, erode, scatter, dissolve |
| Order | law, measure, pattern, hierarchy | Arbiter, Magistrate, Architect, Lawgiver | measured, immutable, exact; bind, align, decree |
| Chaos | chance, freedom, anomaly, disruption | Catalyst, Trickster, Liberator, Chanceborn | shifting, unforeseen, mercurial; disrupt, free, transform |
| Life | breath, pulse, birth, growth, appetite | Lifebringer, Progenitor, Healer, Heartkeeper | living, vital, fertile; quicken, renew, propagate |
| Death | ending, silence, memory, passage | Reaper, Mourner, Ferryman, Endkeeper | silent, fading, mortal; end, mourn, still, pass |

Centered vocabulary depends upon relationship:

| Relationship | Vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| Equilibrium | balance, measure, scales, counterweight, stillpoint, poise |
| Synthesis | union, concord, reconciliation, confluence, communion |
| Conflict | schism, division, fracture, discord, inner war |
| Alternation | cycle, turning, season, pendulum, return, succession |
| Suppression | seal, chain, prison, restraint, containment |
| Paradox | contradiction, impossibility, inversion, neither, both |

These are seed concepts rather than exhaustive word lists. Lexicon entries eventually require coordinate, intensity, connotation, grammar, card-family, naming-role, and exclusion metadata.

## Naming by card type

The same Ethos must use different grammar for different cards:

| Card type | Ethos primarily describes |
| --- | --- |
| Plane Seed | Foundational condition or cosmic principle |
| Land | Character, ecology, or history of a place |
| Structure | Form, authority, or symbolic purpose |
| Facility | Operational function |
| Creature | Nature, behavior, or ecological role |
| Hero | Reputation, choice, or personal identity |
| Leader | Doctrine, authority, or command style |
| Squad | Collective identity and discipline |
| Relic | Legacy, maker, purpose, or historic deed |
| Action | Player intention |
| Battle Event | Something occurring during battle |

For Order + Life, examples include *Genesis of the Measured Garden*, *The Ninefold Orchard*, *Pruned-Antler Guardian*, *Alwen, Keeper of the First Seed*, *Third Cohort of the Living Wall*, *Muster the Garden Host*, and *The Ranks Take Root*.

For a centered Champion of Balance, examples include *Field of Equal Seasons*, *Ilyra, Warden of the Turning Gate*, *The Counterweight Cohort*, *Restore the Measure*, and *The Balance Turns*.

## Universe-bound doctrine

Factions, religions, and divinities are intended to derive from the Universe seed so a Universe retains a recognizable mythology across Eras. Their exact generation is future work. Divine–Infernal remains separate from Ethos: a Divine power may be tyrannical, an Infernal power may defend equilibrium, and a Champion of Balance need not belong to either provenance.
