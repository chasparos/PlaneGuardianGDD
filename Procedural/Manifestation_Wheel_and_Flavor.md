# Manifestation Wheel and Flavor

- **Status:** Foundational design with flavor guidelines
- **Related decision:** [DD-0011](../Decisions/DD-0011-manifestation-wheel.md)
- **Related documents:** [Lore Map and Affinity Wheels](Lore_Map_and_Affinity_Wheels.md), [PlaneGuardian GDD](../GDD/PlaneGuardian_GDD.md)

## Purpose

Manifestation describes how a card exists and what binds its presence to the world. It does not describe elemental substance, moral alignment, intention, or magical technique.

```text
Embodied    <-> Incorporeal
Rooted      <-> Wandering
```

## Axes

### Embodied–Incorporeal

This axis measures dependence on a stable physical form.

- **Embodied:** occupies physical space, possesses a stable body or structure, and normally interacts directly with material objects.
- **Incorporeal:** does not require a stable material body and may exist as spirit, memory, dream, pattern, aura, or presence.

Incorporeal does not automatically mean invisible, invulnerable, or Aether-aligned.

### Rooted–Wandering

This axis measures dependence on a particular locus.

- **Rooted:** identity or power is bound to a place, host, source, territory, or anchor.
- **Wandering:** identity persists across relocation and does not require one permanent anchor.

Rooted does not necessarily mean motionless. A guardian may patrol an entire forest while remaining metaphysically bound to it.

## Primary regions

### Embodied + Rooted — Foundation

A stable physical presence bound to a locus.

- **Examples:** fortress, monument, ancient tree, installed machine, territorial colossus, physical seal.
- **Concepts:** foundation, pillar, bastion, hearth, throne, bulwark, enduring body.
- **Roles:** Foundation Keeper, Pillar Guardian, Rooted Sentinel, Keeper of the Threshold.
- **Mechanical tendencies:** zone occupation, durability, adjacency, local auras, forced-movement resistance, source dependence, limited expedition use.

Foundation vocabulary must not automatically imply Earth. An Embodied and Rooted card may be made from Ice, Water, Shadow, Radiance, or solid Aether.

### Incorporeal + Rooted — Imprint

A nonphysical presence attached to a locus or anchor.

- **Examples:** haunting, ward, ley-line consciousness, ancestral presence, battlefield memory, relic-bound curse.
- **Concepts:** imprint, echo, memory, presence, haunting, resonance, vigil, lingering will.
- **Roles:** Echo, Watcher, Remembered One, Bound Presence, Unseen Warden, Keeper Without Form.
- **Mechanical tendencies:** local triggers, persistence without physical occupation, anchor dependence, specialized targeting, possession or projection within a domain.

A Rooted card should identify its derived anchor type. Possible anchors include a location, Land, Structure, Relic, entity, lineage, oath, Event, or Plane Seed. Anchor type is not another wheel.

### Embodied + Wandering — Pilgrimage

A stable physical identity capable of carrying itself elsewhere.

- **Examples:** creature, Hero, Squad, caravan, migrating forest, walking fortress, mobile construct.
- **Concepts:** pilgrimage, journey, migration, march, caravan, procession, bearer, traveling host.
- **Roles:** Wayfarer, Pilgrim, Walker, Bearer, Marching Host, Pathkeeper.
- **Mechanical tendencies:** tactical movement, expedition eligibility, formation participation, attachment carrying, physical interception, transportation.

Wandering does not imply Chaos. A marching legion may be completely Order-aligned.

### Incorporeal + Wandering — Drift

A mobile presence without a stable physical body.

- **Examples:** ghost, dream, astral current, roaming curse, prophecy, disembodied intelligence, living song.
- **Concepts:** drift, current, whisper, apparition, passage, dream, unbound voice, fleeting shape.
- **Roles:** Drifter, Wandering Echo, Dreamwalker, Unbound Presence, Traveler Between Forms.
- **Mechanical tendencies:** unusual movement, following or attaching to targets, projection, bypassing some physical obstructions, specialized spiritual or mental interactions.

Incorporeal and Wandering do not automatically grant unrestricted movement. Abilities and delivery rules determine which boundaries the card can cross.

| Region | Concepts |
| --- | --- |
| Embodied | body, vessel, form, shell, frame, flesh, incarnation |
| Incorporeal | spirit, thought, memory, pattern, voice, aura, reflection |
| Rooted | anchor, domain, bond, threshold, territory, locus, home, source |
| Wandering | journey, migration, passage, path, exile, pilgrimage, seeking |

## Center and relationships

A low-Salience center means Manifestation is ordinary or irrelevant. A high-Salience center requires a relationship that explains why neither side dominates.

- **Equilibrium:** maintains a controlled boundary between physical and incorporeal existence.
- **Synthesis:** exists physically and incorporeally at the same time, such as a rooted body projecting a wandering spirit.
- **Alternation:** phases, manifests, dissolves, roots, or unmoors over time.
- **Conflict:** body, spirit, domain, or desire to travel oppose one another.
- **Suppression:** one manifestation is imprisoned inside another.
- **Paradox:** violates ordinary locality or embodiment, such as a place that wanders or a body that exists nowhere.

These relationships consume Complexity because mechanics and presentation must explain the contradiction.

## Optional Focus

Manifestation does not need Focus on ordinary cards. Complex cards may use:

- **Embodiment:** emphasizes physical–incorporeal state;
- **Locus:** emphasizes Rooted–Wandering identity;
- **Threshold:** emphasizes movement between manifestation states;
- **Journey:** emphasizes changing anchors or locations;
- **Holistic:** treats body, spirit, anchor, and travel as one identity.

Focus constrains special interpretation rather than creating a normal naming branch.

## Card-type interpretation

| Card type | Manifestation primarily describes |
| --- | --- |
| Plane Seed | Stability, materiality, and locality of the Plane |
| Land | Whether place is fixed, mobile, remembered, or projected |
| Structure | Physical construction, conceptual structure, or mobile installation |
| Facility | Whether operation is local, portable, projected, or host-bound |
| Creature | Body type and relationship to territory or migration |
| Hero | Incarnation, projection, possession, exile, or domain bond |
| Leader | Whether command depends on presence, territory, or projection |
| Squad | Material formation, spectral host, territorial guard, or roaming company |
| Relic | Solid or incorporeal form and whether it binds to place or bearer |
| Action | Where and how an intervention manifests |
| Battle Event | Whether an occurrence remains local, moves, follows, or phases |

## Counterexample test

Holding Elemental Affinity constant must still produce distinct identities:

| Element | Foundation | Imprint | Pilgrimage | Drift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earth | *Basalt Foundation* | *Memory of the Mountain* | *Pilgrim Colossus* | *Unhoused Stone Ancestor* |
| Aether | *Crystallized Aether Engine* | *Vigil of the Ley Nexus* | *Mana-Forged Pilgrim* | *Astral Drifter* |

Earth does not determine embodiment, and Aether does not determine incorporeality.

## Removal test

Removing Manifestation must leave questions that other wheels cannot answer:

- Does the card possess a stable body or occupy physical space?
- Is it tied to a place, host, or source?
- Can its identity survive relocation or join an expedition?
- Does its effect remain where created or follow a target?
- Can destroying an anchor remove it?

If Elemental Affinity, card type, or World Relation answers these automatically, those systems have improperly absorbed Manifestation's responsibility.

## Soft correlations

Earth may lean Embodied or Rooted, Aether may lean Incorporeal, Lands may lean Rooted, entities may lean Wandering, Preservation may lean Rooted, and Transformation may support Alternation. These are tendencies rather than requirements.

The guiding rule is:

> Elemental Affinity describes what resonates within a card. Manifestation describes how that card is present in the world.

## Open questions

- Exact anchor types and severance behavior.
- Movement and targeting consequences of each region.
- Whether manifestation state can change during battle or Plane simulation.
- Complexity costs for projections, phase changes, and multiple simultaneous presences.
- Exact vocabulary metadata, cultural grammar, and visual treatment.
